FEATURE:       Loyalty / CRM Program
PRIORITY:      P1-High (top-3 retention ask in Indian market)
MARKET:        Petpooja charges add-on; POSist has built-in advanced CRM.
               Owners ask "how do I bring customers back?" before buying.
EFFORT:        5-7 days
DEPENDENCIES:  CustomerProfile entity exists (V63). Bill customer fields exist.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PLAN
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Identify customers by phone number at billing (already captured).
2. Auto-enroll every paying customer into loyalty (zero-friction).
3. Earn: 1 point per Rs.100 spent. Redeem: 1 point = Re.1, min balance gate.
4. Visit tracking: milestone rewards (e.g. 10th visit -> free item) optional v2.
5. Points ledger must be server-owned (anti-abuse), synced to Android read-only.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SOLUTION (design)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Tables:
  loyalty_accounts   (id, restaurant_id, customer_profile_id UNIQUE(restaurant,customer),
                      points_balance INT, lifetime_spent NUMERIC, created_at, updated_at)
  loyalty_ledger     (id, restaurant_id, account_id FK, bill_id NULL,
                      delta INT (+earn/-redeem), reason EARN/REDEEM/EXPIRE/ADJUST,
                      created_at)
  loyalty_settings   (restaurant_id PK, earn_rate_per_100 NUMERIC,
                      redeem_rate INT points->Re.1, min_redeem_balance INT,
                      is_enabled BOOL)

Server:
  LoyaltyService
    - earnForBill(bill): called on bill finalization next to InventoryService hook;
      idempotent via ledger unique(bill_id) where reason=EARN
    - redeemAtBilling(restaurantId, phone, points): called by BillingViewModel
      before settle; validates balance; writes negative ledger row atomically
    - balanceOf(restaurantId, phone)
  LoyaltyController (/loyalty):
    GET  /settings | PUT /settings            OWNER-gated
    GET  /balance?phone=                      staff-readable
    POST /redeem {phone, points}              during billing
    GET  /customer-history?phone=             visits + spend

Android:
  - PaymentStep: if enabled + phone entered -> show available points,
    "Redeem X pts (-Rs.Y)" toggle.
  - Settings > Loyalty: enable/disable, set rates.
  - Reports later: top customers by lifetime spend.
