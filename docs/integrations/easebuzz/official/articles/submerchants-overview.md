Sub-merchants are the merchants who get onboarded on the Easebuzz platform by a Master Merchant to manage sub-merchants’ payments. Sub-Merchant can be a distributor or franchise who provides goods or services to the end users and have entered a contract with the Master Merchant which can be an agency.

Sub-merchant arrangement will offer you hassle-free way to accept payments with efficiency and convenience.


## Features
---

- **Quick setup:** A simple onboarding application form and KYC verification are all you need to start accepting payments. Merchant can add a sub-merchant from the Easebuzz Dashboard or using API’s. <br></br>
- **Account Access:** Sub-merchant is also a registered Easebuzz merchant, it will have the ability to create and manage transactions using its own account credentials. Sub-merchants can collect payments, create refunds, and view settlements on via API and Dashboard access.<br></br>
- **Services:** Sub-merchant account gives you access to almost all of the services you expect from a merchant account. Easy collect and Smart Pay services will be default available in the sub-merchant account.<br></br>
- **Charges:** Sub-merchant model is cost-effective, you are charged only when you accept online payments, rather than paying different types of fees including set-up fees, recurring monthly fees. Merchant can charge commission on different types of sub-merchant transactions.<br></br>
- **Settlement:** Every sub-merchant will have own settlement account to collect settlement amount.<br></br>
- **Reporting:** Merchant can track the sub-merchant transactions on a single dashboard. Multiple reports available in both merchant and sub-merchant account.<br></br>
- **Security:** KYC verification of each sub-merchant before onboarding and before they start transacting.<br></br>
- **Control:** Merchant will be responsible for creating and resetting sub-merchant password and can also initiate link transactions for the sub-merchants’ customers from the dashboard.



> **Note:** To on-board the sub-merchant, merchant will request the Easebuzz operations team to enable sub-merchant module. 

## Steps
---

- **Step 1:** Capture Sub-merchant details and submit. 

- **Step 2:** Configure sub-merchant transaction charges, Bank details, Business details and KYC documents and **“Save”**. Request will be sent to the Easebuzz team and team will verify the KYC details of the sub-merchant. If all the details are correct and no discrepancy is observed then sub-merchant will be approved and status of the sub-merchant will be updated as **“Active”**. Merchant can view the list of newly added sub-merchants with KYC and activation status.

- **Step 3:** Once the sub-merchant is approved, he will receive the credentials and login URL and will start transacting.
