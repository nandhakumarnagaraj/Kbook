# POS
---

Easebuzz POS system offers a comprehensive, user-friendly payment collection solution for offline transactions. We aim to bridge the gap between biller systems and POS devices, ensuring real-time transaction updates and efficient payment processing.


## Features
---

- **Seamless Integration:** Seamless billing system integration (PC-POS) with Easebuzz API’s. <br></br>
- **Real-Time Updates:** Instant transaction reflections in POS machine. <br></br>
- **Efficient Payments:** Direct POS transaction initiation. <br></br>
- **Multiple Payment Modes:** Magnetic strip, IC cards, NFC, 1D/2D codes, UPI, EMI. <br></br>
- **Secure Transactions:** ATEX certified for hazardous areas. <br></br>
- **High-Speed Processor:** Android 9.0, quad-core 1.4 GHz. <br></br>
- **Long Battery Life:** 7.4V 2600mAh, 600+ receipts, 240+ hours standby. <br></br>
- **Versatile Scanner:** Fast 1D/2D barcode recognition. <br></br>
- **Settlement Tracking:** Track transactions, T+1 settlements tracking. <br></br>
- **Refunds & Disputes:** Merchant can void the transaction from the device, & disputes will come through acquirer further Easbuzz will raise it to the merchant. <br></br>
- **User-Friendly:** Easebuzz White Label solution with Intuitive Easebuzz App interface. <br></br>
- **Secure:** Advanced encryption certified by PCI-DSS. <br></br>
- **Custom Receipts:** Branded receipts - Easebuzz receipts along with merchant Brand name. <br></br>
- **Sales Analytics:** Detailed collection analytical collection view payment mode wise. <br></br>
- **24/7 Support:** Continuous assistance from acquirer team.<br></br>


## Transaction Flow
---

- Customer selects the payment mode on the POS machine.
- Merchant selects the card and amount on the POS machine
- Customer will insert/swipe or Tap the card in the POS machine to proceed the payment.
- Once the payment is processed, merchant and customer receipt is printed from the POS machine.
- Merchant can check the transaction status.
