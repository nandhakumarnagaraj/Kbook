SmartPay is a faster and easier way of collecting payments from your customers. It stands out because it lets the merchant to handle the look and feel of the payment pages along with the adding customization like redirection URLs, selection of payment modes, unique URL suffix and much more without having any dependency on EaseBuzz.

-	Smart transactions
- Easy Payment
- Less dependencies

## Features
---

- **Create link:** Merchants can create and share multiple links using dashboard.

- **Share Payment Link via WhatsApp, Email, SMS:**  Send the link for payment collection via Whatsapp, Email or SMS.

- **Payment notification:** Get notified for each payment via Webhook, Email, or SMS.

- **Sub-merchants:** Link can be created for individual sub-merchant as well.

- **Payment mode selection:** You can select the subset of payment modes through which payment needs to be initiated.

- **Single link, multiple paymnets:** Link once created can be sent to multiple vendors or customers.

- **Customization:** Add fields as per your requirement on pre-payment and post-payment pages.


## SmartPay Flow
---

![Smartpay.png](https://stoplight.io/api/v1/projects/cHJqOjEyODU1Nw/images/lRVYlPETM9Y)

- To create a SmartPay link, click on **"Create Smart link"** button under **"Smart Pay"** tab.
- Design the look and feel of the payment page.
- Add fields on the pre-payment page to collect payments as per your requirement.
- Configure the settings for the links (payment modes, link name, suffix).
- Add fields on the post payment page (if required).
- Payment link can be shared with your customer via email, WhatsApp, and SMS.
- Once payment is done by the customer, funds will be settled directly in your settlement bank account.
