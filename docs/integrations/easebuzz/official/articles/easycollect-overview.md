Accepting payments from customers is now just a click away. EasyCollect is link-based payment solution, which enables merchants to share payment links automatically to the customers via an email, SMS, WhatsApp, chatbot etc and collect payments for the same instantly. EasyCollect do the hard work for you with its auto reminders and outbound calling capability.

## Features
---

- **Create Payment Links link using APIs & Dashboard:** Create single and Bulk payment links and share with customer for hassle free payment collection using our safe best in class API’s or through Dashboard. <br> </br>
- **Share Payment Link via WhatsApp, Email, SMS:** You can now send payment links for collection via WhatsApp, Email or SMS. <br> </br>
- **Customize Templates:** Customize WhatsApp message, SMS templates as well as IVR messages to reach out to customers for collections. <br> </br>
- **Configure Automated Reminders:** Send automated reminders to the customers via WhatsApp, Email, SMS & Outbound IVR. <br> </br>
- **Payment notification:** Get notified for all your payments via Webhook, Email, or SMS. Webhook functionality will help you get real-time updates on payment status updates. <br> </br>
- **Communicate with your customers keeping Brand Value Intact:** Plugin your existing SMS, Email, WhatsApp service provider for communicating with the clients. <br> </br>
- **Track payments with insights:** Track and manage payments with a single dashboard. Issue refund, get insights on single click. <br> </br>
- **Collect amount with range:** Collect payment between a range by defining minimum amount and maximum amount. <br> </br>
- **Create a shareable payment page:** Custom Fields with Templated Validations. Collect information relevant and important to your business by adding and modifying fields with multiple predefined validations with no coding. <br> </br>
- **Easy to integrate:** EasyCollect comes with easy-to-integrate powerful APIs that your developers will have fun integrating.


## EasyCollect Flow
---

Easy collect enables you to create payment links for your customers manually or can also be generated via API integration. 

![Easycollect flow-100.jpg](https://stoplight.io/api/v1/projects/cHJqOjEyODU1Nw/images/W9BUoo9qxU8)


- To create an Easycollect payment link, click on **"Create link"** button under **"Easy Collect"** tab.
- You can create single or bulk payment links.
- Enter all the mandatory fields to create the easy collect link.
- Save the preferrences for the links as per your requirement.
- Payment link can be shared with your customer via email, WhatsApp, and SMS. SMS, Email and WhatsApp template can be customized as per merchant request.
- Customers can choose any payment mode provided by Easebuzz like cards, netbanking, UPI, wallets and many more to make payment.
- Once payment is done by the customer, funds will be settled directly in your settlement bank account.


## Settings
---
- Configure field names and reminder settings from EasyCollect settings tab.
- Webhook URL settings can be managed from account settings tab.
- Reminders will be sent to the customer as per configured days before expiry date.

> **Note:** Links with unpaid and expired status can be updated. Customer will not be able to make the payment for the link which is already paid.
