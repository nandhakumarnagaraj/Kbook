# Sub-merchants Overview
---

Sub-merchants are the merchants who get onboarded on the Easebuzz platform by a master merchant to manage sub-merchants’ transfers and collections. Sub-merchant can be a distributor or franchise who provides goods or services to the end users and have entered a contract with the master merchant which can be an agency. 

Sub-merchant arrangement will offer you hassle-free way to make payments and collection with efficiency and convenience.


## Features
---
- **Quick setup:** A simple onboarding application form and KYC verification are all you need to start accepting and making payments as a sub-merchant. 
- **Account Access:** Sub-merchant is also a registered Easebuzz merchant, it will have the ability to create and manage transactions using its own account credentials.  
- **Services:** Sub-merchant account gives you access to almost all of the services you expect from a merchant account. 
- **Charges:** Sub-merchant model is cost-effective, you are charged only when you accept online payments, rather than paying different types of fees.
- **Settlement:** Every sub-merchant will have own settlement account to collect settlement amount.
