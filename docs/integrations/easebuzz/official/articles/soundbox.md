# Soundbox
---

Receive instant audio alert for every payment received with Easebuzz Soundbox. A portable audio notification device with UPI QR for your digital payment collection needs. Easebuzz Soundbar comes with SIM card for better network connectivity.

Receive payments with instant audio confirmation. Get the Easebuzz Soundbox delivered to your doorstep.

## Features 
---

- **Multilingual support:** Voice notifications are available in 6 regional languages for digital payment collections. <br></br>

- **Daily settlements:** Receive daily settlements on the same day with soundbox. <br></br>

- **Detachable C-type charger:** Easebuzz SoundBox comes with a detachable C-type charger. <br></br>

- **Best battery life:** Get a robust battery life of 4 days with Easebuzz Soundbox. <br></br>

- **Multi-functional keys for easy operation:** Power on/off button, volume up/down, function/menu buttons helps in ease of use. <br></br>

- **User Manual / Guide:** Comes with a detailed user manual for better usage of SoundBar. <br></br>

- **Immersive Audio Quality:** Enjoy crystal-clear sound and immersive audio quality that elevates audio content to a whole new level. <br></br>

- **Last transaction update:** Missed listening last payment confirmation? Replay the last transaction audio by long pressing the volume up button (3 seconds). <br></br>
