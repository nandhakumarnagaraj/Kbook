# Go-live Steps
---

- **Merchant onboarding**: Create your merchant account and complete the merchant onboarding process. <br><br>
- **Test Integration Kit/Sandbox credentials**: Your sandbox credentials will be sent to your registered email address.<br><br>
- **Complete the Integration**: Choose the payment gateway integration method that best suits your business requirements and complete the integration using the sandbox credentials.<br><br>
- **Execute & Verify Test Cases**: Test your integration using the test cases available on your dashboard in "**Test Mode**". Once you have executed all the test cases, upload the test results for verification.<br><br>
- [**Click here**](https://easebuzzpub.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/integration_test_cases_sample.xlsx) to download the test case sheet template. <br></br>
- [**Click here**](https://easebuzz.stoplight.io/docs/payment-gateway/wlt03odp7gzk5-testing-credentials) for Sandbox test cards and credential details.

![Sandbox.png](https://stoplight.io/api/v1/projects/cHJqOjEyODU1Nw/branches/YnI6OTg0MDU3OA/images/%2Fassets%2Fimages%2FSandbox.png).


- **Live Kit/Credentials**: Replace your sandbox credentials with the live credentials and perform a test transaction in the production environment. Once production testing is complete and the integration is verified, you can start accepting payments from your customers.

![go-liveflow.png](https://stoplight.io/api/v1/projects/cHJqOjEyODU1Nw/branches/YnI6OTg0MDU3OA/images/%2Fassets%2Fimages%2Fgo-liveflow.png)


> **Note:** Configure the required webhooks on your Easebuzz dashboard to receive transaction-response notifications on your server.
