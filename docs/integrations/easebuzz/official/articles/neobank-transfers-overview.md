# Payouts
---

Easebuzz Payouts helps you to disburse funds into your vendors account from your virtual account or current account instantly without any hassle using payment modes like **RTGS**, **NEFT**, **IMPS**.

Easebuzz payouts provides:

- [**Instant transfers**](Instant-Transfers.md)
- [**Scheduled transfers**](Scheduled-Transfers.md)
- [**Payout links**](2.2.3-Payout-Links.md)
- [**Vendor payouts**](2.2.4-Vendor-Payouts.md)
- [**GST payments**](2.2.5-GST-Payments.md)
- [**Tally integration**](2.7-Tally-Integration.md)
