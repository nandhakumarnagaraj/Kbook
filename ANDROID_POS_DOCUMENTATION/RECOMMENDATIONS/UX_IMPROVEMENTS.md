# UX & Operational Recommendations

### Recommendation: One-Tap Quick Tender on Step 3 Payment

**Recommendation ID:** REC-001  
**Module:** Payments & Checkout  
**Category:** UX  
**Priority:** P2  
**Current Observed Behavior:**  
On Step 3 (Complete Payment), the cashier must tap the payment mode dropdown, select Cash or UPI, and then tap `Payment Successful`.

**Problem:**  
In high-volume restaurant billing (e.g. lunch hour or fast casual counters), cashiers settle 80%+ of bills via Cash or UPI. Having to navigate a modal dropdown slows down throughput by 2-3 seconds per ticket.

**Recommended Solution:**  
Introduce prominent quick-tender pill buttons (`[Cash]`, `[UPI QR]`, `[Card POS]`) directly above the payable amount for instant 1-tap mode selection.

**Business Benefit:**  
Faster queue clearance during peak hours and fewer mis-taps.

**Status:** PROPOSED
